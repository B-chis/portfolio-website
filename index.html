About me

Thank you for visiting my profile page. I am currently a software developer in training in Techtonica's 2026 cohort program. I am learning all the fundamental tools to be a software developer. My background is in theatre. My goal is to link my creative experience with the technical and logical tools I develop within this program.

My background includes over a decade in the theatre performance as well as other diverse hospitality roles. I have expereince working with various ages including specialities when it comes to youth and elderly in memory care.

I aspire to use my developing skills in tech in hopes of combining them with work that involves advocacy.
